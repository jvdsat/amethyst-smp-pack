Q: Any recommendations?
A: Use /rtp to randomly teleport around the map to explore. 
   You may die in the world border but this should be no problem.
   Use /home to teleport back to your base
   Claim chunks on the map to mark your base
   Configure your keybinds with the studious keybind mod. It shows a keyboard GUI with unmapped and mapped keys.
